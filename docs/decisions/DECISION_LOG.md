## Decision closure

Decisions D-019 through D-023 supersede the original model-family restriction without changing the U-Belt scope, the mandatory deterministic fallback, or the rule that no unverified model may influence routing.

Decisions D-024 through D-027 authorize the citizen-to-station workflow described in [the implementation plan](../workflows/CITIZEN_STATION_WORKFLOW.md). They supersede the older role-simulation and manual-dispatch decisions for the active local candidate without changing historical acceptance evidence.
